Method and limits

Stratified 75/25 split (n=426 train, n=143 held out). Scaling and mutual-information feature selection (top 10) run inside 5-fold cross-validation to avoid leakage. The default threshold was chosen on training data for at least 95% sensitivity, then evaluated once on held-out data. One small public dataset and one split: this demonstrates the method and is not clinical evidence.
